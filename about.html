God is Almighty

Our Mission

Igniting hearts to walk boldly with Christ, Firestarters empowers young disciples to cultivate a vibrant spiritual presence in their daily lives. We are committed to nurturing authentic faith journeys where each person becomes a vessel of God's transformative love. Together, we kindle the divine spark within, creating a community where the Spirit's guidance illuminates our path and inspires us to spread that light throughout the world.

Beliefs

  • THE LORD IS GOD - THE LORD IS ONE

    We believe that the Bible clearly teaches salvation is a gift of God’s grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ. As Romans 10:9 says, “If you confess with your mouth, ‘Jesus is Lord,’ and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.” This means that salvation is not earned by good works or personal effort, but comes by placing our trust in Jesus, acknowledging Him as Lord, and receiving the new life He offers.
