About FireStarters

Formed in his presence.
Sent in his love.

We are becoming a community whose devotion to Jesus shapes how we worship, grow, serve, and lead.

Our mission

Igniting faith that becomes a way of life.

FireStarters empowers young adults to walk boldly with Christ and cultivate an active awareness of his presence in everyday life.

Through Scripture, worship, prayer, fasting, and authentic friendship, we nurture disciples who carry God’s transforming love into their homes, workplaces, church, and city.

How we live

A community built around practice, not performance.

01

Seek Jesus

We make room for the presence of God through worship, Scripture, prayer, and fasting.

02

Grow Together

We choose honest friendship, mutual encouragement, and a shared life of discipleship.

03

Serve Generously

We lead the next generation and collaborate with others for the flourishing of our city.

What we believe

Rooted in the good news of Jesus.

We believe in one God—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—worthy of our worship and the center of all life.

We receive the Scriptures as God’s trustworthy Word, guiding what we believe and how we live.

We believe salvation is a gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ—not earned through effort or good works.

We depend on the Holy Spirit to transform our character, empower our witness, and guide us in serving others.
