For the next generation

Helping children know they are deeply loved.

Our children’s ministry provides a nurturing place where young people can grow in their understanding of God’s love. Through Bible stories, worship, conversation, and engaging activities, we help children build a strong spiritual foundation.

The same young-adult community that gathers as FireStarters also serves here—learning to lead with patience, joy, responsibility, and care.
