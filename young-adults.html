Life together

A faith we practice side by side.

We come together to study Scripture, worship, pray, fast, share meals, and encourage one another. Whether faith is familiar to you or you are beginning to ask new questions, you are welcome here.

Our goal is not simply another weekly event. We want to become people whose life with Jesus changes how we lead, work, create, build relationships, and serve.
